# Exploratory data analysis

Generated by `scripts/run_eda.py`. All numbers below are computed from the integrated panel (59,170 yield records, 185 countries, 14 crops). Figures are in `reports/figures/eda_*.png`.

EDA uses the full period, including the later test years; it was used to understand the data, and the feature definitions were fixed by prediction-time availability rules rather than by test-period fit.

## 1. Yield distribution and crop differences

- Yields span five orders of magnitude and are strongly right-skewed (skewness 2.9 raw, -0.10 after log transform). Models therefore work in log space.
- Median yield by crop (hg/ha): Buckwheat 9,632, Hops 13,500, Oats 17,169, Maize 17,298, Rye 19,517, Wheat 20,000, Barley 20,066, Ginger 51,356, Garlic 61,250, Sweet potatoes 66,901, Onions, shallots, green 131,062, Potatoes 134,180, Cauliflowers and broccoli 140,832, Carrots and turnips 192,721.
- Root and vegetable crops (carrots, potatoes, cauliflowers, onions) have ~5-10x the per-hectare mass of cereals, so pooled MAE in hg/ha is dominated by those crops.

## 2. Yield trends over time

- Median per-series log-linear trend (series with >= 10 years), % per year: Cauliflowers and broccoli 0.44, Sweet potatoes 0.46, Hops 0.67, Onions, shallots, green 0.91, Garlic 0.95, Ginger 1.06, Buckwheat 1.10, Oats 1.17, Carrots and turnips 1.22, Rye 1.23, Barley 1.30, Wheat 1.38, Potatoes 1.63, Maize 1.66.
- Yields grow slowly and steadily for most crops (technology / management trend), so last year's yield is a strong predictor; the forecasting problem is mostly about year-to-year deviations.

## 3. Geography

- Only country identifiers exist (no regions, farms or coordinates). Between-country differences are large: see `eda_wheat_by_country.png` (wheat 2007-2016).

## 4. Climate trends

- Median country temperature anomaly vs 1901-1960 averaged 1.00 deg C in 2007-2016; linear trend 1961-2016 0.222 deg C/decade (p = 8e-22).
- Median rainfall anomaly trend 1961-2016: -0.06 %/decade (p = 0.8).

## 5-6. Rainfall, temperature and yield

- Same-year rainfall anomaly vs yield log growth (pooled): rho = 0.031 (p = 5.7e-13).
- Same-year temperature anomaly vs yield log growth (pooled): rho = -0.014 (p = 0.0016).
- Between-country wheat yield level vs annual rainfall: rho = 0.018 (p = 0.16); vs mean temperature: rho = -0.418 (p = 2.3e-243). These cross-sectional correlations are confounded by income, irrigation, soils and technology and must not be read as climate effects.
- First-difference panel regression (change in log yield on change in rainfall / temperature anomaly, crop and year fixed effects, country-clustered SE):
  - pooled: +0.41% yield per +10 pp rainfall anomaly (95% CI +0.21 to +0.62, p = 8.3e-05); -0.68% per +1 deg C (95% CI -1.47 to +0.11, p = 0.093); n = 54,139.
  - crop-specific estimates with p < 0.05 (26 tests, no multiple-comparison correction): Barley d_rain10 +0.93%; Barley d_temp -2.10%; Maize d_rain10 +0.90%; Oats d_rain10 +0.81%; Oats d_temp -2.08%; Rye d_rain10 +0.81%; Wheat d_rain10 +0.76%.
  - Associations are small relative to the year-to-year noise in yields; country-average calendar-year climate is a noisy proxy for growing-season weather in cropping regions, which attenuates estimates towards zero. Full table: `reports/climate_association.csv`.

## 7. Pesticides and yield

- Maize yield vs national pesticide tonnes (same year, between and within countries): rho = 0.460 (p = 2.5e-205). Pesticide tonnes scale with country size and agricultural intensity, so this mostly reflects development level.
- Lagged change in pesticide use vs yield change: rho = -0.004 (p = 0.54) (n = 26,627). No meaningful within-country association is visible.

## 8. Predictor correlations

- 31 predictor pairs have |Spearman rho| > 0.9 (lagged log yields with rolling means; same-year vs lagged and baseline climate). Importance scores will be shared among these.

## 9. Missing data

- Raw files contain no missing cells; missingness arises from integration: pesticides start in 1990 and are absent for 28 yield countries; climate ends in 2016; yield series have gaps and different start years.
- Missing share among modelling rows: pesticides_growth_lag1 11.6%, log_pesticides_lag1 8.1%, trend_4y 5.1%, log_y_lag3 2.5%, roll5_std_log 2.5%, growth_lag1 1.3%, log_y_lag2 1.3%, roll3_mean_log 1.2%, roll5_mean_log 1.2%, dev_from_roll5 1.2%.
- Missing values are imputed inside the model pipelines (median + missing indicator, fitted on training folds only).

## 10. Outliers and data-entry errors

- Year-on-year log growth has kurtosis 47.5; 4.2% of changes exceed +/-65% (|log| > 0.5) and 0.77% exceed a factor of e (~2.7x).
- See `reports/data_quality_report.md` for flat runs (likely FAO estimates) and extreme jumps.

## 11. Does the time series support forecasting?

- 1,269 (country, crop) series; median length 59 years; 902 series have >= 30 observations.
- Lag-1 autocorrelation of year-on-year growth is -0.353 (p = 0): shocks partly reverse the following year (mean reversion), which a model using lagged growth and rolling means can exploit.
- Annual data, one observation per series-year: a one-year-ahead forecast for known (country, crop) series is supported; seasonal or sub-annual forecasting is not.
