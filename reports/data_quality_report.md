## Aggregate / historical areas

- FAO `China` is an aggregate (mainland + Taiwan + Hong Kong + Macao); wheat 2010: China 47,484 vs China, mainland 47,486 hg/ha. It is excluded to avoid double counting; World Bank `China` is matched to `China, mainland`.
- Historical FAO entities (USSR, Czechoslovakia, Yugoslav SFR, Ethiopia PDR, Sudan (former), Belgium-Luxembourg, Serbia and Montenegro) have no climate series and are excluded from the modelling panel.
